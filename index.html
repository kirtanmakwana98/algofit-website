SMART FITNESS • REAL RESULTS

Why Fitness Calculations Matter?

ALGOFIT helps you understand your body using simple, practical fitness calculations so you can make better decisions about your daily health and training.

⚖️

Understand Your Body

BMI, body fat, BMR and lean body mass calculations help you understand important aspects of your body and fitness condition.

🔥

Manage Your Calories

Knowing your calorie requirements can help you plan your food intake according to goals such as weight loss, maintenance or muscle gain.

🥩

Track Your Protein

Protein plays an important role in muscle repair and growth. ALGOFIT helps you estimate your daily protein requirement and discover protein-rich foods.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Water is essential for normal body functions and physical performance. Use the water calculator to estimate your daily hydration needs.

REAL-LIFE USE

From Numbers to Better Decisions

Fitness is not only about exercising harder. It is also about understanding your energy needs, nutrition, hydration and recovery. ALGOFIT brings these calculations together in one simple platform so you can use the information in your everyday fitness routine.
